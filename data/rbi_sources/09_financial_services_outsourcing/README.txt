      Directions on Managing Risks and Code of Conduct in Outsourcing of
      Financial Services by NBFCs
      RBI/2017-18/87; DNBR.PD.CC.No.090/03.10.001/2017-18
      Issued November 09, 2017. 13 pages.
      Manifest entry: RBI-FS-OUTSOURCE-NBFC-2017 (source_status: downloaded).

TWO THINGS TO KNOW BEFORE CITING IT:

1. This is NOT the document recorded as RBI-FINANCIAL-SERVICES-OUTSOURCE.
   That entry is the 2006 instrument addressed to BANKS, whose applicability
   explicitly EXCLUDES NBFCs. This 2017 circular is addressed to NBFCs. They
   are different instruments with opposite entity scope and are deliberately
   kept as separate manifest entries.

2. Its manifest entry carries is_current: false. Whether a 2017 circular is
   still the operative instrument for NBFCs has NOT been independently
   verified, and a document must never be presented as current merely
   because it is present. The substantive Directions are in its Annex.

Placing a file here is not enough: the matching manifest entry in
app/rbi/corpus_manifest.json must have its source_status set to 'downloaded'
and its local_path filled in, or nothing may be cited from it.

Every other document in this domain remains unobtained, and nothing may be
cited from a document that is not present.
