      Manifest entry: RBI-FRAUD-NBFC-2024 (source_status: downloaded).

THIS IS THE NBFC INSTRUMENT, ONE OF THREE. RBI issued three parallel fraud-risk
Master Directions on the same day: one for commercial banks and AIFIs, one for
cooperative banks, and this one for NBFCs including HFCs. The other two are
different instruments and are NOT represented in this corpus. Do not cite this
document for a bank or a cooperative bank.

LAYER-SPECIFIC SCOPE. Several chapters apply specifically to NBFCs in the
Upper and Middle Layers (NBFCs - UL & ML). That scope is stated clause by
clause inside the document and is not modelled in the manifest, so read the
clause before concluding it binds a Base Layer NBFC.

EXTRACTION NOTE. pypdf occasionally reorders lines around footnote markers in
this document, so a small number of clause attributions may be imprecise.
Verify the page before quoting.
