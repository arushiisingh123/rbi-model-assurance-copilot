      Master Direction - Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial
      Companies - Credit Information Reporting) Directions, 2025
      RBI/DOR/2025-26/349; DOR.FIN.REC.No.268/20.16.056/2025-26
      Issued November 28, 2025. 70 pages.
      Manifest entry: RBI-NBFC-CREDIT-INFO-REPORTING-2025
      (source_status: downloaded).

THIS IS THE NBFC-SPECIFIC INSTRUMENT. It is NOT the generic Reserve Bank of
India (Credit Information Reporting) Directions, 2025 dated January 6, 2025
(RBI/DoR/2024-25/125), which was obtained earlier and deliberately NOT
ingested. The two are different documents with different entity scope: using
the generic one as the NBFC source would attribute requirements to NBFCs from
an instrument written for a wider set of entities, and the citation would look
perfectly well-sourced while being wrong about who it binds.

This folder was created only once the correct NBFC-specific instrument was
obtained, rather than being filled with the nearest available document.

Placing a file here is not enough: the matching manifest entry in
app/rbi/corpus_manifest.json must have its source_status set to 'downloaded'
and its local_path filled in, or nothing may be cited from it.
